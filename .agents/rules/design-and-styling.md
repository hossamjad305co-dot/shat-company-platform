# SHAT Platform - Design System & UI/UX Standards

## 1. Palette Tokens
- **Brand Navy:**
  - Primary text / Headings: `--shat-navy-950` (`#0a0f1d`)
  - Deep dark surfaces: `--shat-navy-900` (`#111827`)
  - Elevated cards & dark borders: `--shat-navy-800` (`#1f2937`)
  - Soft surface inputs: `--shat-navy-100` (`#f1f5f9`)
- **Shat Emerald Green:**
  - Primary CTA & active highlights: `--shat-green-700` (`#047857`)
  - Border highlights & simulator borders: `--shat-green-500` (`#10b981`)
  - Soft background pills & badges: `--shat-green-100` (`#dcfce7`)
- **Corporate Amber:**
  - Locks and warnings: `--shat-gold-600` (`#92400e`), background `#fffbeb`, border `#fde68a`
- **Page Background:**
  - Reduced-glare calm off-white: `--bg-page` (`#f8fafc`)

## 2. Typography
- **Arabic (RTL):** 'Cairo' for headings (weights 700, 800), 'Tajawal' for body and paragraphs.
- **English (LTR):** 'Outfit' for titles, 'Inter' for body.
- Line height: minimum 1.7 for paragraphs to ensure comfortable scanning.

## 3. Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1240px):** Full horizontal navigation, 3-4 column card grids, side-by-side course details.
- **Medium & Tablet (769px - 1239px):** Collapse horizontal nav into drawer, bottom nav bar enabled.
- **Mobile (<= 768px):** Single column vertical stack (`100%` width), header compressed to `58px`, mobile bottom tab bar active, top notice bar hidden.
