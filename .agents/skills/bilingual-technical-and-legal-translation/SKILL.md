# Bilingual Technical, Legal & Academic Translation (English-Arabic)

## Overview / نظرة عامة
**English:** Translates complex legal agreements, patents, technical documentation, and scientific papers between English and Arabic with rigorous terminology precision and stylistic fluency.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Legal Terminology Alignment**: Translating indemnification, jurisdiction, force majeure, and covenants with exact civil/common law equivalents.
2. **Technical & Engineering Localization**: Localizing software, API documentation, and engineering specifications without losing technical meaning.
3. **CAT & Translation Memory Best Practices**: Utilizing structured glossaries, regex terminology matching, and segment alignment.
4. **Cultural & Stylistic Nuance**: Ensuring appropriate formal tone in Arabic (فصحى) avoiding literal calques and ambiguous phrasing.
